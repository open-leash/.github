## 🐾 Why OpenLeash exists

AI agents are becoming part of how we build software.

They write code.  
They run commands.  
They touch files.  
They connect to tools.  
They make decisions faster than humans can follow.

That power is exciting — but it also creates a new kind of anxiety:

> **“What exactly is my agent doing right now?”**

OpenLeash exists to answer that question.

Not with fear.  
Not with hype.  
Not with another black-box dashboard.

But with **clarity**, **control**, and **trust**.

---

## ✨ The idea

**OpenLeash is a control layer for AI agents.**

It helps developers and organizations understand where agents are running, what they are doing, which tools they are using, and when a human should step in before something risky happens.

Think of it as a calm command center for your agents.

Not a cage.  
**A leash.**

## 🚀 Who OpenLeash is for

### 👩‍💻 Developers

For people running Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, OpenCode, custom agents, and whatever comes next.

You want speed — but you also want to know when your agent is about to do something dumb.

### 🛡 Security teams

For teams that need visibility, policies, audit trails, and safer adoption of AI agents across the company.

Not **“ban agents.”**  
Not **“trust everything.”**  
Something practical in the middle.

### 🧩 Builders of the agent ecosystem

For people building tools, MCP servers, automations, workflows, and agent platforms.

OpenLeash wants to become part of the shared safety layer for this new world.
